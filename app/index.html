Objective

Estimate wildfire probability in the Basque Country in real time by combining Euskalmet weather stations, Open-Meteo data, and a Random Forest model, then display it as a continuous heatmap across the region.

Coverage and Monitoring

Real-Time Environmental Conditions

Forest Areas

As wildfires become increasingly common across the country, proactive anticipation is vital. Euskadi Fire Risk leverages a machine learning model trained specifically on local Basque data to predict hazards early and prevent catastrophic forest fires from spreading here like in other regions.

Euskalmet Weather Stations

Powered by high-precision telemetry, our platform aggregates real-time weather data from local stations provided by the Basque Government, the Provincial Councils (Diputaciones Forales), and MITECO.

Continuous Heatmap

In response to rising global temperatures and climate change, our continuous spatial heat map uses a Random Forest model to analyze current weather conditions, helping the region adapt to and prepare for future fire risks.
